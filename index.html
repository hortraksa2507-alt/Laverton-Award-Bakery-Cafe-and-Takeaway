Our Story

Laverton's Favourite Local Bakery

Laverton Award Bakery Cafe and Takeaway is a cherished neighbourhood spot known for its authentic New Zealand–style pies, flaky pastry, and generous fillings. Whether you're grabbing breakfast before work or a hearty lunch on the go, we've got you covered.

From pepper steak and butter chicken pies to cream donuts and freshly brewed coffee, every item is made to satisfy. Our friendly team and welcoming atmosphere keep locals coming back — and visitors driving from across Melbourne just to taste our award-worthy pies.

  • Authentic NZ Pies Steak & cheese, pepper steak, butter chicken & more
  • Barista Coffee Great coffee to pair with your favourite pie
  • Fresh Daily Sausage rolls, cakes, slices & sweet treats
  • Kiwi Heritage Traditional New Zealand bakery flavours & recipes
