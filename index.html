Our Story

Laverton's Favourite Local Bakery

Laverton Award Bakery Cafe and Takeaway is a cherished neighbourhood spot known for authentic New Zealand–style pies, flaky pastry, and generous fillings. From early-morning tradies to weekend families, we serve the community with warmth and quality.

Our self-service display is stocked daily with savoury pies, fresh sandwiches, cakes and slices. Pair your pie with a barista coffee and experience why locals — and visitors from across Melbourne — keep coming back.

  • Authentic NZ Pies Big chunks of meat, rich gravy, flaky pastry
  • Barista Coffee The perfect companion to any pie
  • Baked Fresh Daily Pies, rolls, cakes, slices & donuts
  • Kiwi Heritage Traditional NZ bakery recipes & flavours
